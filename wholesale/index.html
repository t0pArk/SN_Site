Logistics that go the extra mile

Unique to other niche growers, we have the capabilities for direct-to-store delivery for customers who don’t want to hold product in distribution centers. Our Federal Express program allows us to harvest and deliver product within three days, guaranteed. A minimum of four or five cases per location is required. We work closely with all of our logistic companies to insure that the freshness and quality of the products you receive are to our high standards

Santa Cruz Nursery & Farm

We are a wholesale grower of select crops suited to the microclimates of California’s Monterey Bay. With operations across seven farms and more than 140 acres, our specialties include several varieties of cypress and the rare ventricosa heather. Many of the nation’s top grocers and home centers have trusted us for highly differentiated products, innovative merchandising solutions and superior logistics that result in exceptional sell-through rates.
