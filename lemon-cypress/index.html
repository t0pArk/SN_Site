Lemon Cypress

Native to California’s Monterey Peninsula, this fast-growing, lemon-scented plant has multiple uses in the home, patio and landscape. It will keep its conical shape until 30 feet tall.

  • Up to 30 feet tall and 20 feet wide.

  • In the garden, plant in well-drained soil in early spring before the heat of summer, or plant in early fall. Prune if needed for hedges or to keep smaller. For container growing, transplant to a larger pot to allow for growth and easier water management.

  • Partial shade in hot areas. Full sun in moderate coastal zones.

  • 0℉ to 105℉. USDA Zones 7–10.

  • Keep well watered but not soggy. Can dry quickly in the pot. Be careful to avoid excessive drying or plant may not recover.

  • During spring and summer growth, feed with a balanced fertilizer.
